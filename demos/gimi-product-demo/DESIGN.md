## Style Prompt

Gimi is a dark, glassy agentic commerce interface for local rentals. The video should feel like a live product demo inside a futuristic but practical app: soft black canvas, subtle blue-violet glow, acid-lime action color, glass panels, crisp mono labels, and product cards that feel close enough to click. Motion is smooth and deliberate, with the Gimi orb acting as the central agent.

## Colors

- Background: `#05070d`
- Panel: `#111722`
- Soft panel: `#1a202c`
- Text: `#f4f7fb`
- Muted text: `#8b93a3`
- Accent: `#ceff00`
- Cyan glow: `#73d9ff`
- Violet glow: `#7c6dff`
- Success: `#6bd49b`

## Typography

- Primary: Inter, system sans-serif
- Labels: Geist Mono, SFMono-Regular, monospace

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use generic SaaS gradients or blue-purple template cards.
- Do not make it feel like a slide deck.
- Do not hide the product flow behind abstract claims.
- Do not use busy stock footage.
- Do not claim that the agent signs transactions; the wallet always approves final transaction execution.
